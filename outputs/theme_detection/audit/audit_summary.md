# InSight ML — Cluster Quality Audit & Qualitative Evaluation Report

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
This audit provides an in-depth, human-centered evaluation of the 6 unsupervised semantic clusters generated by `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` on the 10,000 Sephora review dataset (`InSight_ML/data/processed/cosmetics/cosmetics_10k.csv`).

While mathematical clustering metrics (e.g. Cosine Silhouette = 0.0560, Inertia = 5,166.6) confirmed that MiniLM separates reviews substantially better than lexical TF-IDF (Silhouette = 0.0098), **automated mathematical metrics alone do not establish business-theme validity**. This audit examines representative reviews across spatial distances (from centroid to perimeter) and unbiased random samples to detect topic contamination, mixed clusters, and semantic leakage.

## 3. Key Qualitative Audit Findings: Diagnosing Observed Contaminations

### A. The "Retinol in Lip Care" Anomaly (Cluster 3)
* **User Observation:** Reviewers noted a clearly unrelated face retinol treatment (`CLINICAL 1% Retinol Treatment`) appearing near the centroid of Cluster 3 ("Lip Care & Balms").
* **Root-Cause Analysis:**
  1. Cluster 3 contains 117+ reviews of the *Laneige Lip Sleeping Mask*. A large proportion of these are **promotional/incentivized free-product reviews** containing standardized boilerplate phrasing:
     > *"received my complimentary [Brand] a couple weeks ago, I love it!! It works great, makes my skin feel soft... Easy to use... Thank you for my free product"*
  2. The `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` dense embedding model places high semantic weight on formulaic phrasing, exclamation marks, and short expressions of generic satisfaction.
  3. Consequently, promotional reviews for unrelated items (retinol treatments, peel pads, facial cotton) that share this formulaic syntax cluster with the complimentary lip reviews rather than their topical product categories.

### B. The Dual-Focus Dilemma in Cluster 2 ("Acne & Skin Clearing")
* **User Observation:** Cluster 2 contains generic skincare, chemical exfoliation, and dark spot/pigmentation feedback alongside acne reviews.
* **Root-Cause Analysis:**
  1. The top products in Cluster 2 include *Dr. Dennis Gross Alpha Beta Extra Strength Daily Peel Pads* (51 reviews), *The Ordinary Niacinamide 10% + Zinc 1%* (47 reviews), and *Salicylic Acid Dots* (42 reviews).
  2. These products are multi-purpose chemical treatments targeting fine lines, enlarged pores, acne blemishes, and hyperpigmentation simultaneously.
  3. In customer language, discussions of "scars", "dark spots", "pores", and "breakouts" exist in close proximity. Calling Cluster 2 solely "Acne" is a human oversimplification of a broader **"Active Treatments, Exfoliation & Problem Skin"** semantic manifold.

### C. Other Cluster Profiles
* **Cluster 0 (Eye Care, 6.95%):** Highly coherent. Specifically captures under-eye skin, eye creams, puffiness, and dark circles.
* **Cluster 1 (Moisturizers & Hydration, 26.48%):** Very coherent. Dominant focus on dryness, barrier repair, and rich creams.
* **Cluster 4 (Scent & Fragrance, 11.08%):** Moderate coherence. Focuses on aroma (sweet, citrus, overpowering odors), but spans multiple cosmetic forms (face mists, scented lip masks, toners).
* **Cluster 5 (Cleansers & Makeup Removal, 11.47%):** Highly coherent. Focuses on daily facial washing, oil balms, and removing makeup without residue.

## 4. Why Automated Metrics Cannot Establish Business Validity
1. **Curse of Centroid Distance:** Proximity to the cluster centroid does **not** guarantee topical purity. The mathematical centroid is merely the mean coordinate vector. Generic, enthusiastic reviews that use common, unspecialized words often sit closer to the global centroid than highly specific domain reviews.
2. **Hard Clustering on Multi-Aspect Reviews:** A single customer review often states: *"I loved the scent and the hydration, but the pump bottle leaked and it broke me out."* Hard $K$-means forces this multi-aspect review into a single bucket, creating artificial spillover across clusters.
3. **No Substitute for Human Domain Audit:** Mathematical cluster metrics measure spatial compact-ness and dispersion, not semantic usefulness. Human audit templates (such as `human_review_template.csv`) are essential before deploying theme detection to client-facing dashboards.

## 5. Audit Deliverables Saved in `InSight_ML/outputs/theme_detection/audit/`
* `human_review_template.csv`: Standardized 120-review evaluation sheet with `review_id`, `cluster_id`, `assigned_theme`, `fits_theme (yes/no/uncertain)`, `suggested_theme`, and `notes`.
* `audit_representative_samples.csv`: 60 reviews sampled across distance percentiles (0% to 90%) to inspect the core, middle, and outer boundaries of each cluster.
* `audit_random_samples.csv`: 60 unbiased randomly sampled reviews (`random_state=42`) across all 6 clusters.
* `cluster_purity_observations.csv`: Structured breakdown of cluster sizes, coherence ratings, primary foci, and identified mixed-topic intrusions.
